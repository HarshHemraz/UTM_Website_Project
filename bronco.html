BRONCO SPORT



Exterior colour: Cactus Gray

Wheels: 17-inch Black High Gloss-painted aluminum wheels with beadlock capability

Upholstery: Black marine-grade vinyl seats with Sandstone stitching

Interior trims: Brushed Bronze dash panel with rubberized tactical switches

Ready For Adventure.
At the heart of the Bronco lies a turbocharged 2.3L EcoBoost® inline‑4 engine, delivering 300 horsepower and 325 lb.‑ft of torque. Built for low‑end torque and high‑altitude performance, it features a twin‑scroll turbocharger, direct injection, and an available 7‑speed manual transmission with crawler gear—delivering confident power whether you’re climbing rocks, fording streams, or cruising the open trail.

Built For The Wild.
Step into the Bronco’s interior—a purpose-built space where durability meets adventure. The cabin features marine-grade vinyl seats that resist water and stains, rubberized flooring with drain plugs, and washable surfaces ready for mud, sand, or water. Tactical switches line the modular overhead panel, while an available 12‑inch touchscreen keeps navigation and trail data at your fingertips. It’s a rugged, functional cockpit designed to take on the elements without sacrificing comfort or capability.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
G.O.A.T. Modes™ (Goes Over Any Terrain) Seven selectable drive modes (Normal, Eco, Sport, Slippery, Sand, Mud/Ruts, Rock Crawl) adjust 4x4 torque split, throttle response, traction control, shift points, and steering feel for optimized off-road and on-road performance.
Available 7-Speed Manual with Crawler Gear The 7-speed manual includes an ultra-low 94.75:1 crawl ratio in first gear (with available 4.7:1 final drive). The dedicated crawler gear (6.59 ratio) enables precise low-speed control without using the clutch.
Advanced 4x4 System w/ Electromechanical Transfer Case Two-speed electromechanical transfer case offers 2H, 4H, and 4L modes. Automatic 4H engagement at any speed. Available front and rear locking differentials with 4.7:1 final drive ratio.
High-Strength Steel Frame & Independent Front Suspension Boxed high-strength steel frame with hydroformed front rails. Independent front suspension (Dana AdvanTEK) provides 10.8" travel. Solid rear axle with multilink coil springs offers 10.7" travel.
