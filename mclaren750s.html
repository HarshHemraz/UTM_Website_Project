The Symphony of Speed.
The power lap begins with a savage roar as the twin-turbo V8 awakens. Acceleration pins you to the seat—a relentless surge of G-force as the horizon blurs. Each corner arrives with razor-sharp precision; the chassis communicates every nuance of the tarmac through the wheel. The brakes bite with ferocious certainty, shedding speed before you explode onto the next straight.

The Driver’s Sanctuary.
Slide into a cockpit crafted around you. The low, focused driving position wraps you in a blend of Alcantara and fine leather, with every surface purposefully angled toward control. Slim carbon fibre pillars offer an astonishing panoramic view, while the driver-centric console places every control at your fingertips.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Monocage III Carbon Fibre Chassis The lightest, stiffest carbon monocoque in McLaren's series production. Features integrated roof and lower sill for easy entry while maintaining exceptional rigidity and minimal weight.
4.0L Twin-Turbo V8 (M840T) McLaren's most powerful series-production V8 at 750 PS and 800 Nm. Features new turbos, increased boost, and flat-plane crankshaft for explosive acceleration and immediate throttle response.
Proactive Chassis Control III Latest hydraulic suspension with cross-linked dampers replaces anti-roll bars. Provides both comfort and agility, adjusting to road conditions in milliseconds for optimal handling.
7-Speed SSG with Inertia Push Dual-clutch transmission with Inertia Push pre-loads next gear during upshifts. Offers seamless comfort shifts and lightning-quick changes in Track mode.
Carbon Ceramic Brakes with Pre-fill Upgraded system anticipates braking, pre-filling calipers with hydraulic pressure. Reduces stopping distances by up to 2 meters from 100 km/h with improved pedal feel.
