MCLAREN GT



Fuel consumption combined: 11.9l/100km

Estimated C02 emissions combined: 270g/km

Color: Papaya Orange

Wheels: 10-Spoke Super Lightweight Wheels

The Driver's Cocoon.
The McLaren GT's cabin envelops you in supple Bridge of Weir leather and Alcantara. Carbon fiber accents gleam under subtle ambient lighting. The low-slung driving position offers a commanding view of the road ahead, while every surface is meticulously sculpted for both luxury and focused performance.

Blade-Light Symphony.
The McLaren GT's headlights are predatory slashes of pure light. These sleek, blade-like LED units cut through the darkness with surgical precision. Their sharp signature is instantly recognizable—a futuristic gaze that announces both formidable performance and avant-garde elegance long before the car arrives.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
MonoCell II-T Carbon Fibre Chassis The structural heart of the McLaren GT is its lightweight, incredibly rigid carbon fibre monocoque. This pioneering architecture forms a strong, safety-oriented passenger cell while dramatically reducing overall weight, forming the foundation for the car's agile handling and impressive power-to-weight ratio.
4.0-litre Twin-Turbocharged V8 (M840TE) The mid-mounted M840TE engine produces 612 PS and 630 Nm of torque. Engineered for both thrilling response and refined cruising, it features a flat-plane crankshaft for sharp throttle response and a distinctive exhaust note, delivering effortless acceleration suitable for grand touring.
Proactive Damping Control Suspension This innovative hydraulic suspension system reads the road ahead, adjusting damper rates preemptively for exceptional control without compromising comfort. It allows the GT to transition seamlessly from compliant long-distance cruiser to poised performance machine at the touch of a button.
7-Speed SSG Transmission The seamless-shift gearbox offers lightning-fast gear changes in Sport and Track modes, yet operates with smooth, almost imperceptible shifts in Comfort mode. It includes both automatic and manual paddle-shift operation, optimizing performance or refinement based on driver preference.
Carbon Ceramic Brakes (CCM) Standard-fit carbon ceramic discs offer immense, fade-resistant stopping power with reduced unsprung weight. The system provides consistent pedal feel and performance in all conditions, from high-speed touring to demanding track use, while offering exceptional longevity.
