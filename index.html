Mustang GTD Laps Nürburgring Faster Than Any Car

Germany’s iconic Nürburgring Nordschleife is the gold-standard for sports car development.
And the 2025 Ford Mustang GTD is the first car from an American brand to complete a lap in under seven minutes. Driven by Multimatic Motorsports driver Dirk Müller, the 815-horsepower Mustang GTD lapped the circuit nicknamed “The Green Hell” in an officially certified 6:57:685.
It’s the fifth fastest time by a stock production sports car according to the Nürburgring’s official records and Mustang GTD is only the sixth vehicle in that class to break the seven-minute barrier.

Behind the Scenes: The BMW M2’s record lap on the Nordschleife.
Excellent engineering, painstaking preparation and a great team with loads of experience – how the BMW M2 achieved the Nürburgring record. The engine’s running, the driver’s behind the wheel. As soon as he gives the sign, the crew are ready to whip the warmers off the tyres, turn the lever rods of the hydraulic jacks in sync to the left and put the BMW M2 back on its wheels so that it can get going. On a flying start the Nordschleife of the Nürburgring. To go out and break records.
