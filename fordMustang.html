RACING SPIRIT..
The EcoBoost® 2.3L turbocharged 4-cylinder engine that powers the Ford Mustang delivers an exhilarating 315 horsepower and 350 lb.-ft. of torque. Designed with a focus on responsive power and efficiency, this high-output engine features a twin-scroll turbocharger for minimal lag and a sport-tuned exhaust that delivers a signature Mustang growl. Its performance-focused engineering provides the accessible thrills and everyday drivability that defines the modern Mustang experience.

The Modern Cockpit.
Step inside to a driver-focused cabin blending sport and sophistication. Black leather seats with contrast stitching hug you in place, while the sleek dashboard is accented by brushed aluminum trim. The intuitive layout places controls at your fingertips, from the responsive touchscreen to the classic toggle switches, creating a space where performance meets everyday comfort.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Independent Rear Suspension An independent rear suspension system with specially tuned springs, dampers, and anti-roll bars delivers precise handling and improved ride comfort. The system is engineered to maintain optimal tire contact and provide confident feedback, whether carving corners or cruising on the highway.
Performance Brakes The Mustang EcoBoost features larger vented disc brakes with performance pads and four-piston fixed front calipers. This setup delivers strong, consistent stopping power and enhanced fade resistance, giving you confidence during spirited driving and daily use.
Selectable Drive Modes Choose from multiple drive modes—Normal, Sport, Track, Snow/Wet, and Drag Strip—to tailor the Mustang's throttle response, steering feel, stability control, and transmission behavior. Each mode adjusts the car's character for different conditions and driving styles.
10-Speed Automatic Transmission The available 10-speed SelectShift® automatic transmission uses precise gear spacing and rapid shifts to optimize performance and efficiency. It includes steering-wheel-mounted paddle shifters for manual control and adapts to your driving style for a responsive, engaging experience.

Sport-Contoured Seats.

The Mustang's performance seats are crafted to hold you securely during spirited drives while offering all-day comfort. Upholstered in premium leather with contrast stitching, they feature bolstered side supports and available heating and cooling. The driver's seat includes power adjustments with memory settings, ensuring your perfect position is just a button away.

Top Speed & Performance.

The Ford Mustang EcoBoost® reaches an electronically limited top speed of 145 mph (233 km/h). Its turbocharged 2.3L engine, combined with advanced aerodynamics and a performance-tuned chassis, delivers confident high-speed stability and acceleration that pushes you back in your seat.
