FORD RAPTOR



Exterior colour: Stone Gray Metallic

Wheels: 17-inch Beadlock Capable Forged Aluminum Wheels in Magnetic Gray

Upholstery: Black Onyx Leather with Gray contrast stitching

Interior trims: Brushed Dark Aluminum with Carbon Fiber accents

Built For Domination.
At the heart of the Ford Raptor lies a twin-turbocharged 3.5L EcoBoost® V6 engine, delivering 450 horsepower and 510 lb.-ft of torque. Engineered for relentless desert-running and high-speed off-road performance, it features advanced turbo technology, enhanced cooling, and a deep, aggressive exhaust note—power that transforms any terrain into your personal proving ground.

Command The Terrain.
Step into the Raptor’s cabin—a rugged yet refined command center designed for both adventure and comfort. Supportive leather-trimmed seats with durable bolsters keep you planted while traversing rough trails. The cockpit is dominated by an available 12-inch touchscreen, surrounded by tough aluminum trim, rubberized controls, and Raptor-specific digital displays that monitor suspension, pitch, and drivetrain status. It’s a space built for capability without compromising on modern comfort and control.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
FOX™ 3.1 Internal Bypass Live Valve Shocks Electronically controlled monotube shocks with position-sensitive damping. Features internal bypass zones and 15.0” front / 15.0” rear travel. Adjusts compression/rebound in ~20ms via ECU input from wheel speed, steering, and pitch/yaw sensors.
Torsen® Limited-Slip Front Differential Helical-gear mechanical LSD with 3.73:1 final drive ratio. Torque bias ratio: 3.7:1. Provides automatic torque vectoring without electronic intervention. Paired with electronically locking rear differential (E-locker).
Terrain Management System™ w/ Baja Mode Seven-mode ECU calibration altering throttle mapping, transmission shift points, 4WD torque split, ESC intervention, and shock damping. Baja Mode disables traction/stability controls, optimizes shift points at >100mph, and firms suspension.
10R80 SelectShift® Transmission Tuning Raptor-specific calibration of Ford-GM 10R80 transmission. Features reinforced clutch packs, revised torque converter lockup schedule, and off-road shift logic. Includes paddle shifters with hold function in manual mode.

Aggressive Form Meets Function.

The Ford Raptor's design is a statement of purpose—wide, muscular fenders flare over massive all-terrain tires, while the bold grille and integrated skid plates announce its off-road readiness. Signature LED lighting cuts a sharp, modern profile, and the high-clearance suspension gives it a dominant stance. From its vented hood to its integrated side steps and available beadlock wheels, every line and angle is engineered for durability, aerodynamics, and unmistakable presence.
