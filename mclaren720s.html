MCLAREN 720S



Exterior colour: McLaren Special Paint - Silica Black

Wheels: Lightweight 10-spoke forged alloy wheels in Gloss Black

Upholstery: Alcantara and Nappa leather interior in Jet Black

Interior trims: Gloss Black Carbon Fibre interior pack

The Carbon Cocoon
Step inside the McLaren 720S and you're enveloped by its focused, driver-centric cockpit. The cabin is a study in purposeful luxury, swathed in Jet Black Alcantara and supple Nappa leather. Every surface is meticulously detailed with gloss black carbon fibre, from the sweeping dashboard to the slim-line switchgear. Deeply contoured sports seats hold you firmly, while the dihedral doors create a dramatic, immersive entry to this technical sanctuary designed for pure performance.

Fuel Economy
According to the EPA, the 720S is rated for 15 mpg in the city, 22 mpg on the highway, and 18 mpg combined. However, we expect its real-world fuel economy to vary drastically depending on how much time it spends with its throttle wide open.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Monocage II Carbon Fibre Chassis The ultra-lightweight carbon fibre structure forms the 720S's core, offering exceptional rigidity and safety while minimizing weight. Its innovative design includes a unique upper structure with slim pillars for unparalleled visibility.
4.0L Twin-Turbo V8 (M840T) Producing 710 PS and 770 Nm, this mid-mounted engine combines explosive power with remarkable responsiveness. Featuring low inertia turbochargers, it delivers instant throttle response across the entire rev range.
Proactive Chassis Control II This advanced hydraulic suspension constantly adapts to road conditions and driving style. It provides both exceptional handling precision and surprising comfort, eliminating traditional anti-roll bars for reduced weight.
7-Speed SSG Transmission The dual-clutch gearbox offers ultra-fast shifts in performance modes while maintaining smooth operation during relaxed driving. It features both automatic operation and manual control via steering wheel paddles.
Aerodynamic Braking The 720S features an innovative airbrake that deploys automatically under heavy braking. It increases downforce by 30% for enhanced stability while also serving as an active rear wing for optimal aerodynamics.

The Engine M840T V8

The first thing to be acknowledged is that McLaren doesn’t actually build the M840T itself (much like the iconic naturally aspirated V12 that sits mid-ship in the original F1). Instead British company Ricardo is responsible for building the M840T, shipping the completed units to McLaren’s Woking headquarters to be placed into cars. That said, the Woking team has as much ownership over the finished product as if they’d bolted it together themselves.
